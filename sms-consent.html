Quotable Wisdom — RememberMe Feature

How SMS Consent Works in Quotable Wisdom

The RememberMe feature in the Quotable Wisdom app allows users to send a personal text message to a known contact (friend, family member, or colleague) on a meaningful date such as a birthday, anniversary, or holiday.

Messages are personal greetings from one individual to another — not marketing, not promotional, and not bulk messaging. The sender personally knows the recipient and has chosen to send them a message.

In-App Consent Checkbox

Before any message is scheduled for delivery, the app requires the sender to check the following consent box:

Shown to the sender inside the app before saving:

✓

I confirm this person has agreed to receive a message from me.

The sender cannot schedule a message without checking this box.

Message Content

All messages are short personal greetings. Example:

"Happy Birthday! Thinking of you today. — John, via Quotable Wisdom"

Messages contain no advertising, no promotional offers, no links, and no unsolicited content. Each message is a one-time send on a date selected by the sender.

Opt-Out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from the sender via Quotable Wisdom. Reply HELP for support. Message and data rates may apply.
